The Vertical Protocol

The 12-Week
Rise Program.

Rise is an automated periodized system. It converts your 1RM data into specific training loads across three evolving neuromuscular blocks.

Phase 1: Strength & Elastic Prep

Building the floor. High-intensity compound loading (85%+) paired with high-volume "Elastic Prep" work. Prepares the tendons for the explosive force of the blocks to come.

Phase 2: Power & Contrast Training

Bridging the gap. Utilizes Post-Activation Potentiation (PAP) by pairing heavy squats with immediate jumps to maximize motor-unit recruitment and force transfer.

Phase 3: Neural Recruitment & Peak Jumps

Maximum velocity. A high-output block focused on reactive "Neural Lower" sessions and max-intent dunk attempts to peak your vertical height for game-time.

Anchor Lift Logic

The system is built on "Anchor Lifts." Enter your primary maxes once, and the routine automatically scales every accessory Split Squat and RDL load for the full 12 weeks.

Historical Maxes

Logs specific milestones for Cleans, Broad Jumps, and Pull-ups.
