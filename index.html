The Methodology

The 12-Week
Vertical Protocol.

Rise isn't just a list of jumps—it is a periodized Google Sheets system built at the intersection of sports science and cutting-edge technology.

Phase 1: Structural Foundations

Focuses on tendon integrity and eccentric loading. Before you can explode, your body must be able to absorb force. Utilizes isometric holds to prep joints for high-impact weeks.

Phase 2: Force Production

Building the "Engine." Based on the Stretch-Shortening Cycle (SSC), we focus on moving heavy loads with intent, raising your strength ceiling to put more force into the ground.

Phase 3: Peak Elasticity

The transformation. Shifting strength into "twitch." Using Depth Jumps and French Contrast Training, we train the nervous system to fire faster and reduce ground contact time.
