Quick Answers

Good To Know.

How do I book?

Text or call Parker with your date, venue, and rough guest count. We send a simple contract and a payment link for the 25% deposit — your date is held as soon as the deposit clears. Payment is often through Venmo.

What's included in the price?

Prices shown are starting estimates — your final quote depends on your date, venue, and guest count. Here's what it covers: the full bar setup, two live bartenders, 3 hours of unlimited hand-mixed drinks, setup, and teardown. No hidden bar fee.

Do you travel?

Yes! Rexburg and Sugar City are included. Idaho Falls, Rigby, and St. Anthony are +$50; Pocatello, Driggs, and Blackfoot are +$125. Farther than that, just ask for a quote.

Allergies & diet options?

Our drinks contain dairy and coconut, so please tell us about any allergies when you book. Many drinks have a diet version too.

Can you do a smaller event?

Absolutely. Our prices are starting estimates, and smaller budgets are welcome — reach out and we'll figure it out.
