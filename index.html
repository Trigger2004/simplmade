The Process

How It Works.

01
Get in Touch & Book a Date
Reach out, we'll get the details sorted — guest count, venue, vibe — and lock in your date with a deposit.
02
We Show Up & Set Up
We arrive early, build out the full bar, and dial in the setup to match your event. You don't lift a finger.
03
Unlimited Drinks All Night
We hand-mix dirty sodas to order for your guests the entire event. Every drink, made fresh. (You can even talk to us about your problems — just like a real bartender.)
04
We Clean Up & Leave
When it's over, we pack everything up and get out of your way. No mess, no hassle on your end.
